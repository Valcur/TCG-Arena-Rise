**Rise TCG** is a French trading card game by Dark Screen Games. Lead your two Summoners, raise their level and manage your Gold and Ether in this unique fantasy multiverse.

Every card from the public sets is available in **English, French, German and Spanish**, along with the official preconstructed decks to start playing right away.

**Players:** 2 or more

Card data and images come from the [official Rise TCG companion](https://risetcgofficial.com/en).
